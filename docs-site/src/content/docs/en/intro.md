# Introduction to CCIFA MatchCore

**MatchCore** is the living, online, sovereign engine that governs every
dimension of sport-coding life under the **Code & Craft International
Federation Association (CCIFA)**.

## What MatchCore does

- Manages **federations**, **5 confederations**, **leagues**, **clubs**
  and **coders**.
- Runs **drafts**, **transfers**, **calendars**, **rankings**.
- Orchestrates **official matches** — played **100% offline** in
  sovereign arenas.
- Produces the **Sovereign Seal**: a chained SHA-256 hash + an Ed25519
  arbiter signature.
- Records every event in the append-only **Sovereign Ledger**.

## Sovereignty

Only official matches are played offline. Every official match is
sealed and recorded immutably. No seal, no result.

## Certified languages

MatchCore is certified in 11 languages:
**EN, FR, ES, IT, DE, HI, PT, JA, ZH, KO, AR**.
