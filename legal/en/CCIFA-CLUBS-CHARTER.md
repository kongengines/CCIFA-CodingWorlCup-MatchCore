# CCIFA Clubs Charter

**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**

> Version v1.0 — English master text.

---

## 1. Club Licence

A CCIFA club licence is required to compete in any official
competition. It is issued for one season and recorded in the
Sovereign Ledger.

## 2. Roster Rules

- Roster size: 5 to 25 coders.
- At least 3 PRO licenses.
- Each coder holds exactly one license.
- National-team caps are tracked per coder.

## 3. Transfers

- Two transfer windows per season.
- Each transfer requires: both clubs' signatures, the coder's
  signature, a medical, and the federation's sign-off.
- Financial Fair Play caps salary-to-revenue ratio at 0.7.

## 4. Financial Fair Play

- Annual audit by the Ethics & Integrity Committee.
- Sanctions: fines, point deductions, transfer bans.

## 5. Sponsors

- Ethical screening is mandatory.
- Prohibited sectors: gambling, tobacco, weapons.

## 6. Youth Academy

- Minimum age 14.
- Youth contracts are recorded in the Sovereign Ledger.
