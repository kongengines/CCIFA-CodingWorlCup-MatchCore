# CCIFA Coders Charter

**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**

> Version v1.0 — English master text.

---

## 1. Coder Licence

Three levels: PRO, SEMI, AMATEUR. Only PRO and SEMI may compete in
official matches. Licenses are season-based and expire on 31 August.

## 2. Obligations

- Respect the rule pack and arbiters' decisions.
- Submit only original work; plagiarism is a disciplinary offence.
- Maintain only one nationality for national-team selection.
- Submit to doping controls (no AI assistance in official matches).

## 3. Craft Domains

Four certified craft domains:
- **Coder** — algorithmic problem solving.
- **Designer** — UX/UI and system design.
- **Maker** — implementation and tooling.
- **Strategist** — game theory, ranking strategy, drafts.

## 4. Ethical Commitments

- No match-fixing, no contact with betting operators.
- Respect the 11 Certified Languages and the diversity of the
  community.
- Report any integrity concern to the Ethics & Integrity Committee.

## 5. Rights

- Fair competition in sovereign arenas.
- Appeal any arbiter decision within 48 hours.
- Have their career statistics recorded in the Sovereign Ledger.
