# CCIFA Arbiters Charter

**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**

> Version v1.0 — English master text.

---

## 1. Office of the Arbiter

Arbiters are sworn officers of CCIFA. They preside over official
matches, sign Sovereign Seals and rule on anomalies.

## 2. Duties

- Assert that the arena is offline before each official match.
- Verify coder licenses and craft domains.
- Supervise the match lifecycle (start, submissions, finish, seal).
- Sign the Sovereign Seal with their Ed25519 key.
- Record every decision in the Sovereign Ledger.

## 3. Rights

- Independence in decision-making.
- Access to all match data and submissions.
- Protection against retaliation for decisions.
- Renewal of accreditation every two years.

## 4. Ethical Commitments

- No contact with sponsors, betting operators or coders outside
  official contexts.
- No disclosure of any pre-match information.
- Recusal in case of conflict of interest.

## 5. Arbiter License

An arbiter license is issued by the Arbitration Committee and
recorded in the Sovereign Ledger. Revocation follows the
Disciplinary Code.
