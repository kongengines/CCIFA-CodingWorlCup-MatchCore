# CCIFA Media Partners Charter

**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**

> Version v1.0 — English master text.

---

## 1. Accreditation

Media partners are accredited by the Sport-Coding Committee.
Accreditation is renewable annually and recorded in the Sovereign
Ledger.

## 2. Media Rights

- Non-exclusive, non-transferable right to report on CCIFA events.
- Live broadcast of official matches is forbidden (matches are
  offline); only sealed replays may be broadcast after the seal.

## 3. Embargoes

- No reporting on arena operations during a match.
- No disclosure of the seal until MatchCore publishes it.

## 4. Content Rights

- MatchCore retains all rights to the Sovereign Seal, the chain hash
  and the canonical match payload.
- Media partners may quote results with attribution.

## 5. Code of Conduct

- Respect the 11 Certified Languages.
- No betting promotion.
- No interference with arbiters or coders.
