# CCIFA Sponsorship Charter

**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**

> Version v1.0 — English master text.

---

## 1. Purpose

This Charter governs the relationship between CCIFA and its
sponsors. It sets the obligations and rights of sponsors and the
ethical commitments they accept by associating with CCIFA.

## 2. Sponsor Obligations

- Respect the Sovereign Seal and never misrepresent a non-certified
  event as CCIFA-certified.
- Submit to ethical screening; prohibited sectors include gambling,
  tobacco and weapons.
- Honour financial commitments within agreed timelines.
- Refrain from interfering with arbiters, coders or the rule pack.

## 3. Sponsor Rights

- Brand visibility on official MatchCore surfaces (dashboard,
  docs-site, worldcup-site).
- Use of the CCIFA sponsor mark, subject to the brand guidelines.
- Hospitality access to sovereign arenas (no technical access).
- Audit reports on the Sovereign Ledger entries they sponsor.

## 4. Ethical Commitments

- Commit to diversity, equity and inclusion across all activations.
- Respect the 11 Certified Languages in any communication bearing
  the CCIFA mark.
- Contribute to the CCIFA youth academy fund.

## 5. Termination

Sponsorship is terminated automatically upon a breach of the
Disciplinary Code or any conduct damaging CCIFA's integrity.
