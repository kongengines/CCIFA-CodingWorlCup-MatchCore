# CCIFA National Teams Charter

**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**

> Version v1.0 — English master text.

---

## 1. Eligibility

A coder is eligible for a national team if they hold a valid PRO or
SEMI license and have not been capped for another nation.

## 2. Squad Rules

- Each national team may select a squad of up to 23 coders.
- At least one coder per craft domain.
- Caps are recorded in the Sovereign Ledger.

## 3. Qualification

- Each confederation organises its own qualifiers.
- The host nation qualifies automatically.
- Top-ranked nations of each confederation qualify for the World Cup.

## 4. Finals Format

- Group stage (8 groups of 4).
- Round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, final.
- Every match is official and sealed.

## 5. Honours

- The winner is crowned World Champion of sport-coding.
- The Sovereign Seal of the final is the most prestigious record in
  the Sovereign Ledger.
