# CCIFA Disciplinary Code

**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**

> Version v1.0 — English master text.

---

## Chapter I — General Provisions

1. This Code governs disciplinary offences in all CCIFA competitions
   and any activity recorded in the Sovereign Ledger.

2. It applies to coders, clubs, arbiters, officials, sponsors and
   media partners.

3. Jurisdiction lies with the Arbitration Committee (first instance)
   and the Ethics & Integrity Committee (appeal).

---

## Chapter II — Disciplinary Offences

4. **Doping** — use of unauthorised AI assistance, prohibited
   libraries or external computation during an official match.

5. **Match-fixing** — any attempt to predetermine the result of a
   match, including bribing arbiters or coders.

6. **Plagiarism** — submitting another coder's solution as one's own.

7. **Network leak** — establishing any network connection during an
   official match in a sovereign arena.

8. **Hardware tampering** — altering the HSM, the arena node, or the
   physical USB courier bundle.

9. **Ethics violation** — harassment, discrimination, or conduct
   damaging the integrity of CCIFA.

10. **Failure to cooperate** with an investigation by the Arbitration
    or Ethics & Integrity Committee.

---

## Chapter III — Sanctions

| Offence | First offence | Repeat offence |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| Doping | 12-month suspension | Life ban |
| Match-fixing | Life ban | Life ban |
| Plagiarism | 6-month suspension | 2-year suspension |
| Network leak | Match void + 3-month suspension | 1-year suspension |
| Hardware tampering | 2-year suspension | Life ban |
| Ethics violation | Warning to 1-year suspension | Up to life ban |
| Failure to cooperate | Fine + 1-month suspension | 6-month suspension |

Fines are denominated in CCIFA units and capped at 1,000,000.

---

## Chapter IV — Procedure

11. A complaint is filed with the Arbitration Committee within 30 days
    of the alleged offence.

12. The Committee notifies the respondent within 7 days; the
    respondent has 15 days to respond.

13. A hearing is held; both parties may present evidence sealed by
    the Sovereign Ledger.

14. The decision is issued within 30 days of the hearing and is itself
    recorded in the Sovereign Ledger.

15. Appeals to the Ethics & Integrity Committee must be filed within
    48 hours of notification.
