## The sovereign final of sport-coding

The **Coding World Cup** is the quadrennial final tournament of the
**Code & Craft International Federation Association (CCIFA)**.

Every World Cup match is an **official match**: played **100% offline**
in a sovereign arena, sealed by a chained SHA-256 hash + an Ed25519
arbiter signature, and recorded immutably in the **Sovereign Ledger**.

## The trophy

The World Champion lifts the **Sovereign Trophy**, whose base carries
the root hash of the final's Sovereign Seal — the most prestigious
record in the whole Sovereign Ledger.
