# About the Coding World Cup

## Origins

The Coding World Cup was established by the **Code & Craft International
Federation Association (CCIFA)** as the quadrennial final tournament of
sport-coding, gathering the best national teams from the five
continental confederations.

## Format

- **Qualification**: each confederation runs its own qualifiers.
- **Group stage**: 8 groups of 4 national teams.
- **Knockouts**: round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, final.
- **Host rotation**: the host rotates among the five confederations
  (AF, AM, AS, EU, OC).

## Sovereignty

Every World Cup match is **official**: 100% offline, sealed, and
recorded in the Sovereign Ledger. The final's Sovereign Seal anchors
the World Champion's title forever.
